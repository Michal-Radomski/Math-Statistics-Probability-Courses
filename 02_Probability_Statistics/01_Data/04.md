Let's walk through calculating the **mean**, **median**, and **sample standard deviation** using a small, simple dataset:

$$\text{Dataset: } \{2, 4, 5, 6, 8\}$$

There are $N = 5$ data points in total. (Notice that the data is already sorted from least to greatest, which makes our job
easier).

---

### Step 1: Calculate the Mean (Average)

The mean is the sum of all values divided by the total number of values.

1. **Add the values together:**

$$2 + 4 + 5 + 6 + 8 = 25$$

2. **Divide by the count ($N = 5$):**

$$\text{Mean} = \frac{25}{5} = 5$$

The mean of this dataset is **$5$**.

---

### Step 2: Find the Median (Middle Value)

The median is the physical middle value when the data is ordered from least to greatest.

1. Look at our sorted dataset: $2, 4, \mathbf{5}, 6, 8$
2. Since there is an odd number of values ($5$), the exact middle is the 3rd number.

The median is **$5$**.

---

### Step 3: Calculate the Standard Deviation

Standard deviation measures how far, on average, each data point is from the mean. We will calculate the **sample standard
deviation** step-by-step:

1. **Find the distance of each data point from the mean ($5$):**

- $2 - 5 = -3$
- $4 - 5 = -1$
- $5 - 5 = 0$
- $6 - 5 = 1$
- $8 - 5 = 3$

2. **Square each of those differences** (this removes negative numbers):

- $(-3)^2 = 9$
- $(-1)^2 = 1$
- $0^2 = 0$
- $1^2 = 1$
- $3^2 = 9$

3. **Sum the squared differences:**

$$9 + 1 + 0 + 1 + 9 = 20$$

4. **Divide by $N - 1$** (Since we are using a sample rather than an entire population, we divide by $5 - 1 = 4$ to find the
   variance):

$$\text{Variance} = \frac{20}{4} = 5$$

5. **Take the square root of the variance** to get the standard deviation:

$$\text{Standard Deviation} = \sqrt{5} \approx 2.24$$

---

### Summary of Results

- **Mean:** $5$
- **Median:** $5$
- **Standard Deviation:** $\approx 2.24$
