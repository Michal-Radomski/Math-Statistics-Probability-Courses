Unlike permutations, a **combination** is a selection of items from a larger pool where **order does not matter**.

For example, if you are dealt a hand of cards or picking a committee of people, getting Alice then Bob is the exact same
outcome as getting Bob then Alice. Because rearranging the items doesn't create a "new" outcome, combinations result in fewer
total possibilities than permutations.

---

### 1. The Combination Formula (Without Repetition)

When you want to select $r$ items from a total pool of $n$ distinct items where order doesn't matter, the formula is:

$$C(n, r) = \binom{n}{r} = \frac{n!}{r!(n - r)!}$$

- $n$ = total number of items in the set
- $r$ = number of items to choose
- $!$ = factorial

> **Notice the difference from permutations:** The combination formula has an extra $r!$ in the denominator. This divides out
> all the duplicate arrangements where the same group of items was counted multiple times in different orders.

**Example:** How many ways can you choose a committee of 3 people from a group of 5 candidates?

$$C(5, 3) = \frac{5!}{3!(5 - 3)!} = \frac{5!}{3! \times 2!} = \frac{120}{6 \times 2} = \frac{120}{12} = 10 \text{ ways}$$

### 2. Side-by-Side Comparison: Permutation vs. Combination

To see how the rule "does order matter?" changes the math, look at choosing 2 items out of 4 $\{A, B, C, D\}$:

| Feature                         | Permutation (Order Matters)                      | Combination (Order Doesn't Matter)  |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------- |
| **Question**                    | How many 2-letter ordered sequences can we make? | How many 2-item groups can we form? |
| **Outcomes counted separately** | AB and BA are **different**                      | AB and BA are the **same**          |
| **Formula**                     | $\frac{4!}{(4-2)!} = 12$                         | $\frac{4!}{2!(4-2)!} = 6$           |
| **List of possibilities**       | AB, AC, AD, BA, BC, BD, CA, CB, CD, DA, DB, DC   | AB, AC, AD, BC, BD, CD              |
