In probability and set theory, **intersections**, **unions**, and **complements** are the three core operations used to
combine and analyze events.

If you are looking at two events (let's call them Event $A$ and Event $B$) within a sample space, here is what each operation
means and how it works:

---

### 1. Union ($\cup$) — "OR"

The **union** of two sets includes all outcomes that are in **either** Event $A$, Event $B$, or **both**.

- **Notation:** $A \cup B$ (read as "$A$ union $B$" or "$A$ or $B$")
- **In Probability:** It represents the probability that at least one of the events occurs.
- **The General Addition Rule:**

$$P(A \cup B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A \cap B)$$

_(We subtract the intersection so we don't count overlapping outcomes twice!)_

**Example:** If Event $A$ is rolling an even number $\{2, 4, 6\}$ and Event $B$ is rolling a number greater than 4
$\{5, 6\}$, their union is $\{2, 4, 5, 6\}$.

---

### 2. Intersection ($\cap$) — "AND"

The **intersection** of two sets includes only the outcomes that are **shared** by both Event $A$ and Event $B$ at the same
time.

- **Notation:** $A \cap B$ (read as "$A$ intersection $B$" or "$A$ and $B$")
- **In Probability:** It represents the probability that _both_ events occur simultaneously.
- **For Independent Events:** If one event doesn't affect the other, you simply multiply their probabilities:

$$P(A \cap B) = P(A) \times P(B)$$

**Example:** Using the same sets above (Even numbers $\{2, 4, 6\}$ and Greater than 4 $\{5, 6\}$), their intersection is only
the number $\{6\}$, because 6 is both even and greater than 4.

---

### 3. Complement ($'$ or $^c$) — "NOT"

The **complement** of an event consists of all outcomes in the sample space that are **not** in Event $A$. It represents the
exact opposite of an event happening.

- **Notation:** $A'$ or $A^c$ (read as "not $A$" or "the complement of $A$")
- **In Probability:** The sum of an event and its complement always equals 1 (or 100%).

$$P(A') = 1 - P(A)$$

**Example:** If the probability of rolling a 6 on a fair 6-sided die is $\frac{1}{6}$, the probability of _not_ rolling a 6
(the complement) is:

$$1 - \frac{1}{6} = \frac{5}{6}$$

---
