Here is how to calculate the **range**, **quartiles**, and **interquartile range (IQR)** using our age frequency dataset
(with a total frequency of $N = 476$).

### Quick Reference: Cumulative Frequencies

- **Age 14:** Freq = 76 (Positions 1 to 76)
- **Age 15:** Freq = 121 (Positions 77 to 197)
- **Age 16:** Freq = 118 (Positions 198 to 315)
- **Age 17:** Freq = 107 (Positions 316 to 422)
- **Age 18:** Freq = 54 (Positions 423 to 476)

---

### 1. Calculating the Range

The range is the difference between the maximum and minimum values in the dataset.

- **Minimum Value:** 14
- **Maximum Value:** 18
- **Range:** $18 - 14 = \mathbf{4}$

---

### 2. Calculating the Quartiles ($Q_1, Q_2, Q_3$)

To find quartiles for frequency data, we locate the positions corresponding to the 25th, 50th, and 75th percentiles using the
total frequency ($N = 476$).

- **First Quartile ($Q_1$) — 25th Percentile:**
- Position: $\frac{N}{4} = \frac{476}{4} = 119\text{th position}$
- Looking at the cumulative frequency, the 119th value falls within **Age 15** (positions 77 to 197).
- **$Q_1 = 15$**

- **Second Quartile ($Q_2$) — 50th Percentile (Median):**
- Position: $\frac{2N}{4} = 238\text{th position}$
- The 238th value falls within **Age 16** (positions 198 to 315).
- **$Q_2 = 16$**

- **Third Quartile ($Q_3$) — 75th Percentile:**
- Position: $\frac{3N}{4} = \frac{3 \times 476}{4} = 357\text{th position}$
- The 357th value falls within **Age 17** (positions 316 to 422).
- **$Q_3 = 17$**

---

### 3. Calculating the Inter-Quartile Range (IQR)

The IQR measures the spread of the middle 50% of the data by subtracting the first quartile from the third quartile.

$$\text{IQR} = Q_3 - Q_1$$

$$\text{IQR} = 17 - 15 = \mathbf{2}$$

---

### Summary of Spread Statistics

- **Range:** $4$
- **First Quartile ($Q_1$):** $15$
- **Median ($Q_2$):** $16$
- **Third Quartile ($Q_3$):** $17$
- **Inter-Quartile Range (IQR):** $2$
